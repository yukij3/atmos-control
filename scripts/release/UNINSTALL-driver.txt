Removing the atmos-control audio driver
=======================================

Quit atmos-control first. Then, in Terminal:

    sudo rm -rf /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL/atmos-control.driver
    sudo launchctl kickstart -k system/com.apple.audio.coreaudiod

Audio devices drop out for about a second while coreaudiod restarts.
If the system output was left on "atmos-control", pick your real output again in
System Settings > Sound > Output.
